# Conversation identity admission

Approved scope: user accepted unresolved-identity isolation and recovery, preserving normal groups/history, then requested implementation. This extends the previously approved direct recovery plan; existing production/release authorization persists.

Invariant: one visible direct conversation per account/peer. A physical room without established identity must never be silently treated as a group row. Strong identity comes from verified/synced association registry, valid m.direct, retained local identity, or explicit room type/direct reservation metadata with an unambiguous local peer. Never infer direct identity from room name or two members alone. Unknown rooms remain in Matrix and history storage, omitted only from ordinary conversation rows, with one generic recovery notice and a functional retry. Group evidence is explicit group state/creation metadata (including legacy app group power-level state keys), not display name. Account-scoped durable identity lets already classified rooms remain available offline and across restarts. Restore associations before projection, snapshot account fencing, and refresh on asynchronous convergence completion prevent intermediate duplicate rows.

No automatic join/leave/delete, key changes, history mutation, or new-room creation is allowed from classification. Projection evidence does not authorize sending: existing canonical and E2EE/member checks remain. Cold installs with missing all identity evidence must wait for synchronized metadata; do not guess and expose duplicate names. App group creation gains explicit type metadata; normal legacy app groups retain recognition from their existing group authority event keys. Completely unclassifiable external/legacy rooms remain visibly pending rather than being discarded. Tests must cover this limit honestly.

Acceptance: missing m.direct plus absent registry, delayed association recovery, reservation before metadata, restart/offline persistence, account isolation, canonical change, multiple groups with equal names/two members, conflicting peer hints, delayed refresh and no room mutations. Unchanged backend gates can be reused only with documented identical inputs; new mobile source needs focused/full Flutter and UI gates, plus repository verify as required.

Production compatibility amendment: read-only audit found one pre-marker room with three current joined members and no direct association/reservation evidence. After every direct/conflict check, complete local membership containing self and at least three current JOINED members is positive multiparty evidence under the app two-party DM invariant. Invitations/knocks and summary/name alone do not qualify. Hydrate the separate local member database without HTTP, do not overwrite fresher in-memory member events, and persist the established group identity for later departures. All unknown two-member/single-member rooms remain pending rather than guessed. Audit198 joined rooms:52 candidates after private/control exclusion;15 explicit groups,1 legacy multiparty,36 single-member unclassified. No production metadata was changed.
