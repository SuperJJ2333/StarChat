The most significant quality/lifetime defect is the cross-instance preparation pin. The code deliberately shares flights, room slots and foreground holds, but _writingSources is local to a store. The controlled Windows probe observes an attempted deletion of a real open .part file, with PathAccessException errno 32. Cleanup must consult shared true preparation lifetime, and release only after the original writer settles. Cancellation of a transport in the same service was correctly guarded in the inspected code: the canceled tombstone is not converted to sent by a late ACK, and the source is retained until the real future ends.
